Finance & Accounts Officer

About the Role

The Finance & Accounts Officer is responsible for supporting T-KEL's financial administration, maintaining accurate financial records, monitoring transactions, and helping management maintain proper financial accountability.

This role requires honesty, confidentiality, accuracy, responsibility, and proper documentation of financial activities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Maintain accurate records of T-KEL income and expenditure.
  • Record and organize approved financial transactions and supporting documents.
  • Monitor payments, receipts, expenses, and other financial records.
  • Assist with reconciliation of financial records and identification of discrepancies.
  • Prepare financial summaries and reports for management when requested.
  • Assist management with budgeting and financial planning.
  • Maintain proper documentation for approved payments and financial activities.
  • Maintain confidentiality of T-KEL's financial information.
  • Report unusual transactions, discrepancies, or financial concerns to the appropriate management authority.
  • Support proper financial procedures across T-KEL projects, productions, and operations.

Requirements

  • Strong honesty and personal integrity.
  • Good numerical and record-keeping skills.
  • Ability to maintain accurate financial records.
  • Good organizational and administrative skills.
  • Ability to work with confidential information.
  • Basic knowledge of accounting or financial administration.
  • Ability to use spreadsheets or other digital tools for record keeping is an advantage.
  • Previous experience in accounting, finance, administration, business, or a related field is an advantage.

Role Rules & Expectations

  • All financial activities must be properly documented.
  • No financial transaction should be falsified, hidden, or deliberately misrepresented.
  • T-KEL financial information must not be disclosed to unauthorized persons.
  • Personal funds and T-KEL funds must be kept separate.
  • The Finance & Accounts Officer must follow approved management procedures for financial activities.
  • Any suspected financial irregularity should be reported to T-KEL Management.
  • Financial records must be maintained accurately and made available to authorized management when required.
  • The officer must act professionally and avoid conflicts of interest in financial matters.
  • Unauthorized use or transfer of T-KEL funds is prohibited.
  • Confidential financial documents must be properly protected.
Important: This application does not automatically make you a member of T-KEL Management. Every application is reviewed by T-KEL Management before approval.
