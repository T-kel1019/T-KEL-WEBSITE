ABOUT T-KEL DISTRIBUTION

Built From T-KEL's Creative Vision

T-KEL Distribution is a music distribution platform being developed by Terriken Entertainment Limited to give artists a structured path from music creation to digital distribution.

Our goal is simple: help artists release their music professionally while building a system that supports rights, royalties, reporting and artist growth.
