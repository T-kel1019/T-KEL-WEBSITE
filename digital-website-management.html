Digital / Website Manager

Join T-KEL Management as a Digital / Website Manager and help maintain, organize and develop T-KEL's website and digital content.

About the Role

The Digital / Website Manager is responsible for helping T-KEL maintain its digital platforms, especially the official T-KEL website and its digital content.

The role involves keeping website information organized and up to date, supporting digital content management and reporting technical or content-related issues to T-KEL Management.

The Digital / Website Manager may work closely with the Production, Music, Distribution, Comedy and other T-KEL teams when their projects require website or digital updates.

Main Responsibilities

  • Help maintain and update the official T-KEL website.
  • Add, update and organize approved movies, music, comedy content and other creative projects on the website.
  • Keep website information accurate and properly organized.
  • Monitor website forms, application pages and other approved digital features.
  • Report website errors, broken features, security concerns or technical problems to the appropriate authority.
  • Assist with publishing approved digital content.
  • Help maintain website images, videos, descriptions, links and other content.
  • Check important website pages periodically to identify problems or outdated information.
  • Assist with digital organization for T-KEL projects.
  • Keep proper records of important website-related tasks when required.
  • Submit digital and website reports to T-KEL Management when requested.

Requirements

  • Must have a good understanding of websites and digital content management.
  • Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS and JavaScript is an advantage.
  • Experience with website management, content management systems, hosting platforms or web development is an advantage.
  • Must be organized and detail-oriented.
  • Must be able to identify and report website problems.
  • Must be willing to learn T-KEL's website management procedures.
  • Must communicate effectively with other T-KEL departments.
  • Must be responsible with access to T-KEL digital platforms.

Role Rules

  • Website changes must follow T-KEL's approval procedures.
  • Major changes to the website must not be made without proper authorization.
  • Login credentials, API keys, passwords, secret keys and other confidential technical information must never be shared with unauthorized persons.
  • The Digital / Website Manager must never publish false, unauthorized or misleading information on behalf of T-KEL.
  • T-KEL website content must be handled professionally.
  • Website files and digital resources must not be deleted, modified or transferred without proper authorization.
  • Any suspected security problem must be reported immediately to the appropriate T-KEL authority.
  • The role must not be used to access, change or disclose information outside the person's assigned responsibilities.
  • Confidential information obtained through the role must remain confidential.
  • The Digital / Website Manager must maintain accurate records of important digital management activities when required.
Important: Appointment to the Digital / Website Manager position is subject to T-KEL Management review, verification and approval. Submitting this application does not guarantee appointment.
