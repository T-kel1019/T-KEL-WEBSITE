About the Founder

Aondoakaa Terhide Kenneth, popularly known as Terriken the shameless producer, is the founder of Terriken Entertainment Limited (T-KEL).

His creative interests extend across film, music, comedy, storytelling, production, editing and entertainment.

His journey is built around creativity, persistence, self-belief and the determination to continue creating even when circumstances are difficult.

Terriken believes that limited resources should not become an excuse to stop pursuing a meaningful dream. When an obstacle appears, the goal is to learn from it, make corrections and continue moving forward.

Persistence

"Persistence is the key."

Persistence is one of the principles that guides Terriken.

Failure is not considered the end of the road. Obstacles are viewed as part of the journey.

When something fails, the lesson is taken from it. When a production does not meet the required standard, it can be discarded, studied and improved upon in the next production.

When people disappoint him, he chooses to see the experience as a way of discovering who can genuinely work with him and who cannot.

Ideas & Creativity

Terriken believes that ideas do not always arrive in a complete or polished form.

An idea may initially appear only as a short thought, a headline or a topic. The responsibility of the creator is to analyze it, expand it and use initiative to develop it into something meaningful.

Because an idea can easily be discouraged before it is developed, Terriken prefers to carefully analyze ideas and build them before allowing outside opinions to determine their value.

Music

Terriken has a strong love for music and is especially attracted to melody, beats, choruses and the overall sound of a musical piece.

Musical ideas can come in different forms, and he believes such ideas should not simply be wasted.

When music is being considered for use in a film or video, however, the meaning of the music is important because it must be appropriate for the production.

His approach to music is therefore strongly connected to creativity, sound and expression.

Film, Comedy & Entertainment

Terriken creates entertainment for different purposes.

Some productions are created simply to make people laugh and bring happiness.

Other productions are created to educate people, create awareness, explain things that may otherwise be difficult to communicate, or explore spiritual and cultural subjects.

As an actor, Terriken is willing to take on different characters and roles depending on the story, including serious, humorous, spiritual and unusual characters.

What Success Means to Terriken

Success is not defined only by money, popularity or applause received directly.

One important measure of success is what people say about you when you are not present.

The good things people remember, the positive things they can say about your work, and the impact you have made on their lives are considered meaningful forms of success.

Recognition is also important: being identifiable by your work, your voice, your presence and the things you have created.

Another sign of success is having people who genuinely want to stand with you and support you when you need help.

The T-KEL Legacy

One of the major reasons for building Terriken Entertainment Limited is the desire to create a lasting legacy.

Terriken wants the name to remain meaningful even after he is no longer here.

The goal is for the company to become associated with good things, positive memories, creativity, entertainment and the people whose lives it has touched.

The company should grow into something valuable enough that future generations will have no reason to erase its origin or forget where it came from.

Faith & Personal Belief

Faith in God is an important part of Terriken's personal worldview.

Terriken believes that God exists and is the creator of heaven and earth.

He also sees science and faith as capable of being understood together, believing that natural processes can occur while God remains the ultimate source behind existence and the conditions that allow those processes to happen.

His understanding of God is personal and deeply connected to what he believes within his heart rather than simply accepting every explanation given by other people.

Freedom & Maturity

Freedom is important, especially as a person grows older and becomes responsible for their own decisions.

However, freedom must be accompanied by maturity. Excessive freedom without sufficient maturity can result in serious consequences.

In this view, true freedom is connected to the ability to understand responsibility and make mature decisions.

People, Family & Friendship

Terriken believes in treating people with respect and kindness, regardless of whether they are family, friends or strangers.

The people closest to us are important, but kindness should not be restricted only to people we already know.

A person should be able to show respect and goodwill to people they meet for the first time as well.

Working With Terriken

Working with Terriken is not primarily about being perfect.

The important questions are: Are you ready? Do you genuinely mean it? Are you willing to receive corrections? Do you believe in yourself?

A willingness to learn, accept correction and keep improving is an important part of working together.

What T-KEL Should Mean

Whenever people hear the name Terriken, the hope is that it will bring a moment of joy, happiness and relief.

The name should remind people of good memories, enjoyment, funny moments, good music, movies and positive experiences.

When someone encounters Terriken Entertainment Limited, the goal is for that person to feel happy, satisfied and valued — not disappointed.
