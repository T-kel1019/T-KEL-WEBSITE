Media Manager

Join T-KEL Management as an Media Manager and help coordinate the day-to-day activities, projects and administrative operations of T-KEL.

About the Role

The Media Manager helps T-KEL Management coordinate its different activities and departments so that approved tasks and projects can move forward in an organized manner.

The role may involve working with production, digital, music, distribution, comedy, finance, security and other approved T-KEL departments.

The Media Manager does not automatically have authority over every department. Responsibilities and authority are based on the person's assignment and instructions from T-KEL Management.

Main Responsibilities

  • Help coordinate the day-to-day operations of T-KEL.
  • Follow up on approved tasks and assignments.
  • Help coordinate communication between departments.
  • Monitor important project deadlines and report delays.
  • Help identify operational problems affecting T-KEL projects.
  • Escalate serious operational issues to the appropriate T-KEL Management authority.
  • Assist with organizing meetings, activities and approved company operations.
  • Help ensure that management instructions are properly communicated to the relevant team members.
  • Follow up on assigned responsibilities and provide progress updates.
  • Assist different departments when coordination is required.
  • Prepare operational reports when requested.
  • Maintain professional communication with T-KEL staff, management and approved partners.

Requirements

  • Must be organized and responsible.
  • Must have good communication and coordination skills.
  • Must be able to follow up on tasks and deadlines.
  • Previous experience in management, administration, project coordination, entertainment or business operations is an advantage.
  • Must be able to work with people from different departments.
  • Must be able to report problems clearly and promptly.
  • Must respect T-KEL's organizational structure and management decisions.
  • Must be dependable and willing to learn T-KEL's operational procedures.

Role Rules

  • The Media Manager must operate within the authority assigned by T-KEL Management.
  • The Media Manager must not make major company, financial, employment or production decisions without proper authorization.
  • Confidential T-KEL information must not be disclosed to unauthorized persons.
  • Management instructions must be communicated accurately and professionally.
  • Operational problems should be reported rather than hidden.
  • The Media Manager must not use the position for unauthorized personal benefit.
  • Company property, documents and information must be handled responsibly.
  • Staff and team members must be treated professionally and respectfully.
  • The Media Manager must keep appropriate records of important operational activities when required.
  • Serious issues involving safety, security, finances, misconduct or major project disruption must be escalated to the appropriate T-KEL authority.
Important: Appointment to the Media Manager position is subject to T-KEL Management review, verification and approval. Submitting this application does not guarantee appointment.
