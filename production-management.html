Production Manager

Join T-KEL Management as a Production Manager and help coordinate the planning, organization and execution of T-KEL movie and creative productions.

About the Role

The Production Manager is responsible for helping T-KEL organize and coordinate its movie productions and other approved creative projects from preparation through completion.

This role works closely with the production team, actors, crew members, directors and other T-KEL management personnel to make sure productions are properly organized and completed.

Main Responsibilities

  • Coordinate production activities before, during and after filming.
  • Help prepare production schedules and communicate important dates to cast and crew.
  • Coordinate with actors, crew members and other departments involved in a production.
  • Help organize filming locations and production arrangements.
  • Monitor production equipment, props and costumes where necessary.
  • Identify production problems and report them to the appropriate T-KEL management personnel.
  • Help ensure that cast and crew members understand their assigned responsibilities.
  • Assist with managing production deadlines.
  • Help coordinate retakes when scenes do not meet the required production standard.
  • Maintain proper communication between production teams.
  • Submit production reports when requested by T-KEL Management.

Requirements

  • Must be responsible, organized and dependable.
  • Must be able to communicate effectively with people.
  • Must be willing to work with actors, filmmakers and production crew.
  • Previous experience in film production, event coordination, entertainment or project management is an advantage.
  • Must be willing to follow T-KEL production procedures.
  • Must be able to work under production deadlines.
  • Must respect T-KEL management decisions and approved production plans.

Role Rules

  • Production information must be treated as confidential where T-KEL Management requires confidentiality.
  • The Production Manager must not independently change major production decisions without proper authorization.
  • Any serious production problem must be reported promptly to the appropriate management authority.
  • Cast and crew must be treated professionally and fairly.
  • Production schedules and deadlines should be respected.
  • T-KEL equipment, materials and production resources must be handled responsibly.
  • The Production Manager must not use their position for personal benefit or unauthorized financial transactions.
Important: Appointment to the Production Manager position is subject to T-KEL Management review, verification and approval. Submitting this application does not guarantee appointment.
